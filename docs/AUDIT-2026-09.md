## 0. The finding

**The site is technically clean and commercially invisible, and those two facts are unrelated.**

Ninety days of Search Console:

| Average position | **24.9** (page 3) |

There is no technical fault to fix. Production returns no `X-Robots-Tag`, no `noindex`, correct apex canonicals on all 54 URLs, valid schema on every page type, 0.2s TTFB, a sitemap Google reads successfully, and 260 healthy internal links with zero orphans. City-page copy is genuinely unique (mean 3% five-gram overlap, worst pair 8.3%), so there is no doorway-page exposure either.

The site is not ranking because **nothing on the internet links to it.** Everything below follows from that.

## 1. The 22 pages Google has never crawled

29 pages are not indexed. The breakdown:

| Reason | Pages | Assessment |

| Page with redirect | 6 | Expected - the `next.config.js` legacy redirects |

Every one of the 22 shows **Last crawled: N/A**. Google found them in the sitemap and declined to spend crawl budget fetching them. A validation attempt has been stuck in "Started" since **7/9/26**, roughly eight weeks.

**Which 22:**

- `/services/` - the core commercial page
- `/service-area/` **and 10 of 11 city pages** - Destin, Fort Walton Beach, Niceville, Miramar Beach, Mary Esther, Navarre, Santa Rosa Beach, Shalimar, Bluewater Bay, Valparaiso *(Sandestin is the only city page indexed)*
- `/equipment/`, `/equipment/first-aid-kit/`, `/equipment/ronzeil-slatmill/` - **the entire affiliate revenue surface**
- `/contact/`
- 6 blog posts: can-you-over-exercise-a-dog, dog-park-not-tiring-dog-out, dog-reactive-on-leash, is-my-dog-overweight, ronzeil-slatmill-build, why-we-record-every-session

Read that list again. A mobile dog-conditioning business serving eleven Florida cities has **no city page in Google's index except Sandestin**, and none of its three affiliate pages.

### Why "Discovered - currently not indexed" happens

It is almost always a crawl-budget verdict, not a quality verdict. Google assigns crawl budget largely on perceived site importance, and the strongest external input to that is inbound links. With zero backlinks and a domain first indexed in June 2026, the site sits at the bottom of the queue. Google crawled the pages it considered core, then stopped.

This is why it is not a content problem. Six of the uncrawled pages are 1,500+ word posts citing real research. `/services/` is a well-built commercial page. Google has not judged them thin - it has not looked at them.

## 3. Google Business Profile - the largest single gap

**Status: created, not verified.**

For a service-area business, this outranks every other item in this document.

- Every top query above is a "near me" query. Those SERPs are dominated by the **local pack** - three map results above the organic results. An unverified profile cannot appear in the local pack at all.
- The local pack typically takes the majority of clicks on "near me" queries. The site is currently competing only for the organic remainder, at position 25.
- A verified profile is also an **entity signal**. It confirms to Google that a real business exists at a real service area with a real phone number, which feeds the trust calculation that governs crawl budget. Verification plausibly helps the 22 uncrawled pages as a side effect.
- It is free, and it is the only item here with a same-week payoff.

## 6. Real defects found

Ordered by value, not severity. None of these are why traffic is flat.

### 6.1 Fixed in this pass
- **`FAQPage` schema was rendering on zero of 10 posts** that wrote answers inline (`**Q?** A.`). The extractor only matched a bolded question on its own line. Five live posts plus five scheduled ones emitted no FAQ schema at all. Fixed, with a regression guard in `npm run check:schedule`.
- **`/privacy/`, `/terms/`, `/contact/` had no `og:image`.** Now generate branded cards through `/og/`.
- **`public/llms.txt` carried 18 em dashes**, which the brand rules ban in all copy, metadata and schema. llms.txt is the one file whose entire audience is machines quoting us verbatim. Fixed.

### 6.2 Open - worth doing
- **Equipment pages carry `Article` schema, not `Product` + `Review`.** These are the affiliate pages. `Product`/`Review` markup is what produces rich results and what AI assistants quote when someone asks "is the Ronzeil slatmill any good." Highest-value remaining schema work.
- **`/book/` has only `AnimalService` + `WebSite`.** No `BreadcrumbList`, no `Service`, no `Reservation`. It is the money page and the least marked-up commercial page on the site.
- **All four `/tools/*` routes render dynamically (`ƒ`)** because they `await searchParams` for the `?embed=1` check. Every crawl and every visit hits the server instead of the edge cache. Moving embed to its own route segment (`/tools/x/embed/`) would make both static.
- **Thin commercial pages.** `/book/` renders 255 words, `/equipment/` 291, `/contact/` 402, `/faq/` 444, `/tools/` 487. `/equipment/` and `/contact/` are both in the uncrawled 22 - not because they are thin, but thin pages give Google no reason to reconsider.
- **828KB of uncompressed JS on a blog post** across 13 chunks, driven by framer-motion and `@studio-freight/lenis` on nearly every route. Not currently hurting - TTFB is 0.2s - but it is the first thing to look at if CWV ever degrades.
- **Sitemap submitted to GSC as `https://kaisrun.xyz/sitemap.xml/`** with a trailing slash while `robots.txt` declares it without one. Both return 200 and Google reports Success, so this is cosmetic.

### 6.3 Deliberately not changed
- **19 page titles exceed 60 characters.** The suffix logic in `lib/blog/post-metadata.ts` already drops the brand suffix when a title would overflow, so these are long editorial H1s, not a bug. Their first 60 characters read as complete hooks. Rewriting them trades brand voice for SERP pixels the site has not yet earned.

## 7. AEO / GEO

This is the strongest part of the site and the reason it is worth fixing the crawl problem rather than starting over.

**Working:**
- `robots.txt` explicitly allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, OAI-SearchBot, Google-Extended and Amazonbot. Most local service sites block these by accident.
- `llms.txt` is 161 lines of genuinely structured entity description.
- Every blog post opens with a bolded Quick Answer sentence, which is the single most citable format for AI Overviews.
- `FAQPage` schema now renders across the post library.
- Sources are named in plain language throughout ("a 2012 Norwegian cohort study", "Marcellin-Little at NC State"), which is what makes a passage safe for an assistant to quote.
- Four free tools with a documented embed mode - the most quotable assets on the site.

**Gaps:**
- Zero `Review` or `AggregateRating` anywhere. Correct today, since there are no reviews to cite. It becomes the top AEO gap the moment the GBP has reviews.
- `Person` schema for Travis exists on `/about/` only. His experience is the E-E-A-T asset; the author box on every post is not marked up as `Person` with `sameAs`.
- No `Product` markup on affiliate pages (see 6.2).
