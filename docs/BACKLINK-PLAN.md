# Kai's Run - First 10 Backlinks

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Current external backlinks: 0.** That number is the direct cause of 22 pages Google has discovered and never crawled. See `docs/AUDIT-2026-09.md`.

The goal is not a link-building campaign. It is getting off zero with links a local service business earns legitimately, in roughly the order of least effort per link.

---

## Tier 1 - citations (an afternoon, no pitching)

Structured business listings. Individually weak, collectively the baseline every local competitor already has. **Use identical NAP everywhere:**

```
Kai's Run
Destin, FL  (service-area business - hide the address, set the radius)
850-218-5855
kaisrunmobile@gmail.com
https://kaisrun.xyz
```

| Where | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Bing Places** | Same Microsoft account as Bing Webmaster Tools. Do both in one sitting. |
| **Apple Business Connect** | Free, fast, and it is how Apple Maps and Siri find you. Almost nobody local bothers. |
| Yelp | Service-area business listing |
| Nextdoor Business | Disproportionately effective for a driveway service |
| BringFido | Dog-specific directory, topically relevant |
| Alignable / Manta / Yellow Pages | Low value individually, cheap to do |

**Do not** buy a citation-blast service. Inconsistent NAP across scraped directories is worse than no citation.

## Tier 3 - local media (the highest-value links available)

A mobile dog gym that brings a self-powered slatmill to driveways on the Emerald Coast is a genuine local story. These outlets cover exactly this kind of thing and they link.

| Outlet | Angle to pitch |
|---|---|
| **Get The Coast** | New local business, visual, unusual equipment |
| **Niceville.com** | Hyperlocal. Also already covers red tide and beach conditions, which your tools address. |
| Destin Life | Local business feature |
| The Destin Log | Same |
| Mid Bay News | Bluewater Bay / Niceville coverage |
| WKRG / WEAR | Long shot, but summer pavement-heat safety is an evergreen local TV segment and you have a live tool for it |

**Best hook:** not "new business opened." Pitch the **pavement heat tool** in summer or the **red tide dog safety** angle in fall. Both are public-service stories with a local expert attached, which is what gets picked up and linked.

## What not to do

- **No paid link packages, PBNs, or guest-post farms.** A brand-new domain with zero organic links suddenly acquiring 50 is the clearest possible spam pattern.
- **No directory blasts.** See NAP note above.
- **No reciprocal-link schemes** beyond genuine local partners you would refer to anyway.

## Measuring it

Check GSC Links monthly (`authuser=1`). The number to watch is not total links, it is whether "Discovered - currently not indexed" starts falling from 22. That is the signal that crawl budget has increased.

Realistically: the first 5 - 10 legitimate local links should move crawl behavior within 4 - 8 weeks. Verifying the GBP in parallel compounds it, because it confirms the business entity at the same time the links confirm its relevance.
