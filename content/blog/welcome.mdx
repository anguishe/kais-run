Kai's Run exists for one reason: high-drive dogs need real work, not a lap around the block. We bring a professional slatmill setup to your driveway so your dog can run with structure, supervision, and safety.

Most “exercise” services stop at tired legs. We focus on controlled output — pace, posture, and recovery — so you see a dog that is settled, not just winded.

## What “mobile gym” actually means

We are not dog walking and we are not daycare. Sessions are athletic: warm-up, working sets on the mill, cool-down, and a short report texted after the visit. You stay close enough to watch, and far enough to let your dog focus.

The equipment travels with us. That means zero loading your dog into a hot car, zero lobby small talk, and zero guessing whether the session was worth the drive.

## Who this is for

If your dog clears the fence for squirrels, spins when the leash comes out, or still has “one more gear” after a long beach day, you already know the gap. Structured conditioning fills that gap without turning your week into a second job.

We work with dogs from about four months up, with rabies vaccination and a completed waiver before the first session. Julius K9 harnesses are on the truck in common sizes — bring your own if you prefer.

## What happens on day one

Your first visit is an intro session: assessment, a Run Profile card, and a progress photo texted to you. You leave with a clear picture of where your dog is today and what a realistic cadence looks like going forward.

From there you can stay on-demand, bundle sessions, or step into a membership if you want priority booking and a steady rhythm.

## The promise we keep

Every dog gets the Tired Dog Guarantee. If your dog is not noticeably tired after a session, we work with them on the next session at no charge. That is not marketing language — it is the standard we hold ourselves to.

If you are in Destin, Fort Walton Beach, or Niceville and you want proof before you commit, book an intro. We will show you the difference structured conditioning makes in one visit.
