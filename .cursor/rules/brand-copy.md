# KAI'S RUN — BRAND COPY & MESSAGING

## Positioning — Use These Exact Frames
- NOT dog walking · NOT daycare · NOT pet sitting · NOT a training class
- IS performance conditioning · IS mobile fitness · IS structured energy regulation
- "The only premium mobile dog gym serving the Emerald Coast."

## Messaging Hierarchy
| Level | Message | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 5-second | Your Dog Deserves to Run. | Hero H1, social bio, van wrap |
| 30-second | Structured canine conditioning. Delivered to your driveway. Not a dog walk. A performance session. | Hero sub, Google description |
| 2-minute | High-drive dogs need more than a walk. Kai's Run brings professional slatmill conditioning directly to your home — energy release, behavioral stability, physical performance. | Services page, email |
| Origin | Travis couldn't keep up with Kai. He found the slatmill. It changed everything. Now he brings it to every dog on the Emerald Coast. | About page, origin video |

## Origin Story (use verbatim or paraphrase closely)
Travis grew up in Destin. His Rhodesian Ridgeback mix Kai had uncontrollable energy — destructive behavior, anxiety, impossible to tire out with normal walks. Travis discovered the slatmill: a self-powered treadmill where the dog sets their own pace. No motor. Nothing forced. Kai ran, then slept. Everything changed. Travis built Kai's Run to bring that same solution to every high-drive dog on the Emerald Coast.

## Voice & Tone Rules
- Athletic, premium, direct — no fluff
- Short sentences. Active voice.
- Never use: "furry friend" · "pup" · "pooch" · "fur baby" · any cutesy pet language
- Use: "dog" · "high-drive dog" · "your dog" · "athlete"
- Numbers add credibility — use them: "3–5× the output of a walk" · "30–45 min sessions"
